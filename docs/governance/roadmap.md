# TIDES Roadmap 2026-2027

The TIDES Roadmap for 2026-2027 is the TIDES Board's strategic roadmap. It describes the direction, priorities, and milestones for the Transit Integrated Data Exchange Specification (TIDES) through 2026, 2027, and beyond, spanning specification development, tooling, community growth, and adoption.

The Roadmap was approved by the TIDES Board on June 10, 2026. The approval is recorded under [Board Actions](actions.md).
